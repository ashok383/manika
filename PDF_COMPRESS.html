✨ Smart Compression
Select a compression level according to your requirement. The program renders PDF pages at different resolutions and JPEG qualities and rebuilds the PDF.

🎯 Target Size:
If you enter a target such as 500 KB or 2 MB, the compressor automatically tries progressively stronger settings until the target is reached or the smallest practical result is obtained.

🔒 Privacy:
Processing takes place in your browser. Files are not intentionally uploaded to a server.

⚠️ Important:
Exact compression depends on the PDF. Scanned and image-heavy PDFs normally compress more effectively. PDFs consisting mainly of text/vector graphics may show less reduction.
