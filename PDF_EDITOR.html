Select a PDF file to start editing.
How to use:
1. Open your PDF document.
2. Choose ADD TEXT and click on the page to insert text.
3. Choose DRAW or SIGNATURE and draw using your mouse or touchscreen.
4. Choose HIGHLIGHT to mark content or WHITEOUT to cover unwanted areas.
5. Use ADD IMAGE to insert a photo or signature image.
6. Download your edited PDF.

Important: This editor adds editable annotations on top of PDF pages. It does not directly modify the original embedded PDF text. To replace existing text, use WHITEOUT over the old text and ADD TEXT over it.
